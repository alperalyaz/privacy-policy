Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Megafon is a Bluetooth megaphone app. It turns your phone into a live microphone and streams your voice to a connected speaker. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Megafon does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

Microphone

The app uses your microphone only while you are holding the talk button. Audio is processed in real time on your device and sent directly to the connected speaker. It is never recorded, saved, or uploaded anywhere.

Network

Megafon does not connect to the internet. It contains no ads and no third-party tracking or advertising libraries.

Children

Megafon is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Permissions

The app requests a single permission: the microphone, used to capture your voice while you hold the talk button. Nothing else — no storage, no location, no contacts, no notifications.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
