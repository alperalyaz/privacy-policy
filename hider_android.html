Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

Hider — Image Scrambler is a fully offline image encryption tool. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • We do not upload or transmit your images to any server
  • We do not store your passwords — they are used in memory only during processing and discarded immediately after
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • We do not access the internet — the app has no network permission

How the App Works

All image processing happens locally on your device. When you select an image and enter a password:

  • The password is converted to a numeric seed via SHA-256 (in memory only)
  • Pixel positions are scrambled or restored using that seed
  • The result is saved to your device storage
  • No data ever leaves your device

Storage Permission

The app requests access to device storage solely to read selected images and save processed results. It does not access unrelated files or folders.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. There is no advertising, analytics, or cloud processing of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Since we collect no data from any user, this app is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be published at this URL with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at encryptimages.com.
